## Opportunity Reviews

### LangGraph Checkpoint Persistence And Cost Diagnostics
- Observed demand signal: production LangGraph users keep reporting persistence loss, replay duplication, and checkpoint bloat.
- Evidence sources and dates: `#7417` (2026-04-05), `#7714` (2026-05-05), `#6792` (2026-02-12), `#6956` (2026-02-26), the agent observability blog (2026-01-27), and a current GoFundMe job posting.
- Repeat pattern: the same family of bugs spans storage bloat, replay loss, interrupted resume paths, and duplicate execution.
- Likely user or buyer: agent platform teams, internal automation teams, and consultants shipping production LangGraph workflows.
- Current workaround or money signal: custom checkpoint tuning, third-party saver packages, LangSmith observability, and public hiring for production agent work.
- Why existing solutions appear insufficient: the docs and blog posts explain how the stack should work, but the issue threads show that common failure modes still appear in real usage.
- One-week test: offer a paid checkpoint audit and deliver a one-page findings memo plus a CLI run against a public demo or sanitized export.
- Falsification threshold: no team wants a paid review, or the audit finds no meaningful difference in storage, replay, or duplication behavior.
- What should not be built: a new agent framework or a full LangGraph replacement.
- Confidence: medium-high. The remaining gap is direct proof that teams will pay for the audit rather than only reading the docs.

### Tailscale Split-Tunnel Routing Exception Diagnostics
- Observed demand signal: users repeatedly want domain, app, or device exceptions to exit-node and DNS routing behavior.
- Evidence sources and dates: `#18378` (2026-01-10), `#15521` (2025-04-03), `#13677` (2024-10-03), `#3506` (2021-12-05), the pricing page, and the other-VPN FAQ.
- Repeat pattern: the same routing pain shows up across Android, macOS, exit nodes, DNS behavior, and App Connector workarounds.
- Likely user or buyer: IT admins, security-conscious teams, and power users managing laptops, phones, and exit-node policies.
- Current workaround or money signal: users disable DNS settings, use App Connector, exclude the browser, or switch VPN products when routing does not fit the workflow.
- Why existing solutions appear insufficient: the workarounds are coarse and platform-limited, and they do not solve the underlying policy-debugging problem.
- One-week test: ask three admins whether a paid routing-policy review would prevent rollout mistakes.
- Falsification threshold: admins say the current workarounds are enough or the pain is too edge-case to budget for.
- What should not be built: a generic consumer VPN client or a full network stack.
- Confidence: medium. The gap is a cleaner paid wedge and a stronger buyer path.

### ArduPilot Telemetry Integrity And Failsafe Diagnostics
- Observed demand signal: operators and developers repeatedly report telemetry loss, logging gaps, and unreliable failover detection in the flight stack.
- Evidence sources and dates: `#32905` (2026-04-24), `#32351` (2026-03-02), the automated log-diagnosis forum thread (2026-03-18), and the Iridium Certus telemetry thread (2026-02-27).
- Repeat pattern: packet loss, telemetry counters, startup failures, and manual log analysis recur as separate reports.
- Likely user or buyer: UAV operators, robotics labs, integrators, and flight-test engineers.
- Current workaround or money signal: manual log review, bench tests, and forum-based troubleshooting, but no public proof of direct spend.
- Why existing solutions appear insufficient: the current tooling exposes symptoms, not a reliable integrity score or a simple go/no-go view for telemetry streams.
- One-week test: ask operators or integrators to upload representative logs for a telemetry-integrity report.
- Falsification threshold: the failures reduce to one-off wiring mistakes and do not repeat as a software or workflow gap.
- What should not be built: autopilot firmware, control laws, or a replacement ground-control station.
- Confidence: medium-low. The next useful validation needs private logs, so this stays on the watchlist.
