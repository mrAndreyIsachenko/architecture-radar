# Opportunity Radar Interests

This file is the authoritative source for opportunity and demand research priorities.

The radar should find evidence-backed opportunities, not generate speculative product ideas. Findings must connect to observed demand, repeated pain, workaround behavior, competitor proof, or ecosystem movement that can be independently checked.

## Topic Scope

Prioritize opportunities related to:

- AI, LLMs, agents, evals, memory, tool use, context engineering, and observability.
- Blockchain analytics, wallets, protocol tooling, indexing, transaction interpretation, and compliance-adjacent workflows.
- VPN, privacy networking, secure access, traffic-control systems, and fleet administration.
- Drones, robotics, autonomy, telemetry, mission operations, simulation, and safety review.
- Document AI, OCR, multimodal extraction, evidence ingestion, and provenance.

Broad topical relevance is insufficient. A selected opportunity must expose a concrete demand signal and a testable next step.

Use these topic families for accounting:

- `ai-llm-demand`
- `blockchain-demand`
- `privacy-networking-demand`
- `drones-robotics-demand`
- `document-ai-demand`

## Evidence Priorities

Prefer signals that show at least one of:

- repeated pain from independent users or maintainers;
- users paying for an imperfect solution;
- teams maintaining scripts, spreadsheets, forks, or manual workflows as workarounds;
- fast-growing open-source adoption with unresolved operational or enterprise needs;
- public job posts, procurement language, or pricing pages showing budget attachment;
- ecosystem shifts that make an old workflow newly urgent.

Do not treat a launch post, model announcement, or viral repository as demand by itself.

## Required Opportunity Shape

Every selected opportunity must include:

- evidence sources and dates;
- the repeated pain or demand signal;
- the likely user or buyer;
- current workaround, competitor proof, or money signal;
- why existing solutions appear insufficient;
- what can be tested in one week;
- what would falsify the opportunity;
- confidence level and evidence gaps.

Do not recommend building a product unless the report connects evidence to a small demand test.
