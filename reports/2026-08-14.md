## Executive Summary

This run adds three source-backed mechanisms and extends two existing patterns:

- `clearideas/agent-runtime` contributes manifest-hashed checkpoint envelopes, attempt-fenced resume, and explicit suspend/fail/cancel lifecycle handling for long-running agent workflows.
- `huridocs/pdf-document-layout-analysis` contributes structural-first PDF analysis with late 200 dpi promotion only for the segments that need richer pixels.
- `microsoft/agent-framework-durable-extension` contributes durable agent sessions, nested child orchestrations, and human-in-the-loop replay gates on top of Durable Task.
- [Evidence-Carrying Execution Envelopes](../patterns/evidence-carrying-execution-envelopes.md) was expanded with durable agent and checkpoint variants.
- [Deferred Image Materialization](../patterns/deferred-image-materialization.md) was expanded with a conversion-service variant that only promotes tables, formulas, and pictures when needed.

The run did not find a strong drones candidate worth selecting. That family remains a gap rather than a forced selection.
