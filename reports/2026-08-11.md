## Executive Summary

This run adds four source-backed mechanisms and extends one existing pattern:

- `opendatalab/MinerU` contributes a windowed PDF conversion pipeline with effort-gated OCR promotion and backend-aware batching.
- `wenet-ec/edge-core` contributes a durable command-execution state machine plus a structured WireGuard health probe.
- `trezor/blockbook` contributes a liveness-capped blockchain sync worker with fork recovery, abort-aware worker coordination, and reorg restart logic.
- `mem0ai/mem0` contributes identity-scoped memory mutation, transactional history, entity relinking, and hybrid semantic/BM25 retrieval.
- [Reorg-Safe Materialization Windows](../patterns/reorg-safe-materialization-windows.md) was expanded with Blockbook as another independent implementation variant.

The run did not find a strong drones candidate worth selecting. That family remains a gap rather than a forced selection.
