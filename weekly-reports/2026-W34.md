## Executive Synthesis
- The week’s architecture evidence split into two clusters: one around recovery-oriented infrastructure for blockchain, networking, drones, and code intelligence on `2026-08-11`, and a second around checkpoint durability, document parsing, and durable agent sessions on `2026-08-14`.
- The strongest cross-file theme remains auditability and recovery in stateful systems: envelopes, checkpoints, resumable workflows, and selective materialization all show up as reusable mechanisms rather than product features.
- On the opportunity side, only LangGraph cleared a sell-before-build threshold. Tailscale routing diagnostics and ArduPilot telemetry diagnostics stayed watchlisted because the public evidence still lacks a clean paid wedge or private-log access path.
